## Cookieless analytics

glotlang.com analytics and glot Insights set no cookie and store neither IP addresses nor visitor identifiers: only daily counters per language, country and event (for example a signup). No consent is required for them.

## Cookies

glot uses a single strictly necessary cookie: the session cookie that keeps you signed in to the dashboard. No advertising or tracking cookies.
